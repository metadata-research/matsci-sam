# Votes, comments, and status

## Voting

Every definition has up and down arrows. The score orders definitions on
a term's page, so the community's preferred definition rises to the top.

## Definition status

Definitions earn standing through votes, shown as a small chip.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| proposed | A new definition, score below 2 |
| community-reviewed | Score of 2 or more |
| stable | Score of 5 or more |

Status is derived from votes at all times, so it rises and falls with
the score. It also appears in the term's machine-readable record.

## Comments

Comments are open on every definition. On AI-generated definitions,
comments do more than discuss: the model reads new comments as feedback
and revises its definition in response. The revision, and the comment
that prompted it, both appear in the term's
[provenance](/docs/provenance).
