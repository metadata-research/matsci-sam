# Adding a term

Sign in with Google, then open **Add** in the navigation bar.

The form asks for three things.

- **Term.** The word or phrase you are defining. If the term already
  exists, your definition is added alongside the others, and readers can
  compare and vote. Each person can contribute one definition per term.
- **Definition.** Name the class of thing, then what distinguishes it.
  For austenite, "A face-centered cubic phase of iron and its alloys,
  able to dissolve considerably more carbon than ferrite."
- **Examples.** One or more sentences using the term in context.

A toggle at the top of the form chooses between two modes.

**Classic.** Your definition is added as written. When a term is defined
for the first time, the site also generates an independent AI definition
for comparison, so readers can weigh a community definition against a
machine-written one.

**Interactive AI refinement.** Your definition is added as written, and
the definition page then offers a Refine button. See
[AI refinement](/docs/ai-refinement) for how the loop works.

After submitting, you land on your definition's page, where you can edit
it, tag it, and follow votes and comments.
