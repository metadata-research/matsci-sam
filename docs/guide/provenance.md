# Provenance

Every term has a provenance page, linked from its definitions list. It
answers the question "where did this definition come from?" in full
detail, as a graph and as a timeline.

The record covers the complete history of a term. Who wrote each
definition and when. Which prompts and models generated which AI text.
What feedback each refinement round used, and how an accepted suggestion
derives from both the model's suggestion and the author's original.
Comments, votes, and edits appear as events. Voter identities are not
shown on the public view.

The graph uses the W3C PROV-O vocabulary. Entities (definitions,
suggestions, feedback), activities (writing, refining, accepting), and
agents (people and models) are drawn as nodes you can click for detail.
The timeline below it tells the same story in order.

Provenance is derived on demand from the site's ordinary records rather
than kept as a separate log, so it cannot drift from the data it
describes. The graph can also be downloaded as PROV-O Turtle from the
provenance page, described in [Metadata access](/docs/metadata-access).
