# Studies

A study gives a community an ordered activity over selected terms. Follow the
study instructions for its purpose, definitions to consider, and closing
questions.

## Help in the study

Select **Study help** to read guidance for the current step. Choose **This
study's instructions** to reread the task. **Back to study** returns to your
unfinished form. Help does not submit an answer or complete a step.

These topics use the same text as this guide. Links open in another tab.
Use **Feedback** for a site problem, the closing question for study feedback,
and a definition comment box for public discussion.

## The study page

Open the study link and sign in with your usual account. Existing community
members can select **Begin study** or **Continue study** and can find the
activity under **Your studies** on their profile.

ID4 round two permits anyone with the link to join while the study is open.
Select **Join and begin study** to join ID4 and enter the activity. Other
studies may require an invitation or membership added by a steward. Contact
the person who shared the link if access is required and no join button appears.
See [Communities and scope](/docs/communities#joining).

The public overview gives the instructions, terms, and closing date.
**About this study** returns to that overview. **The terms** opens the
collection. **Continue study** resumes at the first unfinished step.

## Terms used in the study

| Term                    | Meaning                                                                            |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Term                    | A concept in one vocabulary                                                        |
| Definition or candidate | One contributed interpretation of a term                                           |
| Revision                | One exact version of a definition                                                  |
| Position                | The definition you accept or publish for a term in the study                       |
| Canonical definition    | The highest-ranked definition in the owning vocabulary                             |
| Support score           | Upvotes minus downvotes on the current revision, including votes outside the study |

A Position records your choice. Canonical order follows
[vote scores and tie-breaks](/docs/community#definition-order), which may
change as people contribute. A support score is not a count of study positions.

**Suggest a revision** creates a separate definition with its own history.
An edit by the primary author creates a revision of the existing definition.

## The study activity

ID4 round two has instructions, one Position step for each of eight terms,
and closing questions. You can accept, revise, propose, comment, or skip during
the Position steps. There is no repeated Review round.

Other studies may have a separate Review round for votes and comments.
The progress indicators show completed steps and your first unfinished step.
Later unfinished steps remain locked. A dash identifies a skipped term.
Completed steps can be reopened for reading.

## The Position step

Choose the definition closest to what you consider correct. You may choose
your own definition regardless of its score. Propose a first definition or
skip when none is available.

The earliest model-authored definition appears first when one exists.
Other candidates follow by support score, with older definitions first in a
tie. This presentation differs from canonical order on a vocabulary page.
Use the wording, attribution, and discussion to assess a candidate.

### Accept a definition

**Accept this definition** records the exact revision as your Position and
completes the step. It adds an upvote, changes your downvote to an upvote, or
retains your existing upvote without adding another point. An upvote cast
outside the study does not complete this choice.

### Suggest a revision

Select **Revise this definition**, explain what should change, and request a
language-model draft. Review and edit the result. **Publish revision** creates
a separate candidate credited to you and the model, records your Position,
and completes the step. The original remains available. Publication does not
cast a vote.

**Discard draft** publishes nothing. Before a draft exists, you can accept the
source or return to the definitions. Use **Propose a new definition** to write
your own alternative if model drafting is unavailable.

ID4 round two advances to the next unfinished step after publication. Post
any public comments before completing the Position. Studies with a Review
round allow further votes and comments there.

### Propose a new definition

Select **Propose a new definition** to write an independent candidate for the
term. You may include an example of use. **Publish new definition** records
your Position and completes the step without casting a vote.

The proposal has no replacement target. **Propose a replacement** on an
ordinary definition page is a separate action for superseding another candidate.

### Comment on a definition

ID4 round two has a comment box beneath each definition. Select **Post
comment** before accepting or publishing a Position. The comment saves your
text without requesting a model draft or completing the step. You can still
accept, revise, or propose afterward.

Studies with a separate Review round provide comment boxes there. The thread
may also include discussion from outside the study.

### Skip a term

Select **Skip this term** and confirm if you do not know the term well enough
to choose. Skip before recording a Position or posting study comments.
The skip records no definition, vote, or comment and is final within the
walkthrough. It also skips the paired Review step when one exists.
Skipped steps remain available for reading.

## Reviewing the definitions

This round appears only in studies that include Review. Use the upvote,
downvote, and comment controls, including when only one definition is present.
You may vote on your own definition. Continue when there are no definitions.

Each account has one vote per revision. Select the same arrow again to
withdraw your vote, or the other arrow to change it. **An upvote already
recorded by Accept is withdrawn if you press up again.** A proposal published
in Position has no automatic vote.

Cards begin in canonical order and stay in place while you vote. Review
leaves your earlier Position unchanged. Scores and threads may include
contributions from outside the study.

Votes and comments save when submitted. Select **Done with this term** to
complete Review. A vote on every candidate is not required by the interface.
Follow the study instructions. Completed Review steps are read-only.

## The closing questions

Answer the question shown and select **Submit**. Scale questions accept a
value from 1 to 5 with the displayed endpoint meanings. Text questions accept
a written response. Saved answers can be reread but not edited in the
walkthrough. A study may use its own questions or omit them.

Use **Feedback** to report a site problem.

## Saving and returning

Positions, skips, published definitions, votes, comments, and submitted
answers are saved. Return through the study link or **Your studies**, then
select **Continue study**.

Unsubmitted form text is a draft. **Study help** keeps it on screen, but
navigation or a reload can lose it. In a Review round, saved votes or comments
still require **Done with this term** to complete the step.

**Review completed study** opens your consolidated record of choices, skips,
study votes and comments, and closing answers. Later vocabulary edits or
votes can change wording and scores without changing your recorded choice.

Earlier ID4 positions, skips, and answers still count after removal of the
repeated Review round. Its previous Review activity remains under **Your
earlier responses** in the completed record.

The study stops accepting actions at closing time. Saved contributions remain
in the vocabulary and its [provenance](/docs/provenance).

## Study and vocabulary workflows

Use the activity to record study participation. Ordinary vocabulary pages
use the same definitions and revisions but do not complete study steps.

| Action   | Vocabulary pages                     | ID4 round two                                                  |
| -------- | ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Choose   | Compare and vote                     | Accept, revise, propose, or skip                               |
| Publish  | Add a definition or author revision  | Record a suggested revision or new definition as your Position |
| Vote     | Support, oppose, change, or withdraw | Accept records or retains an upvote. A proposal adds no vote   |
| Comment  | Discuss a definition                 | Comment before completing Position                             |
| Progress | No required sequence                 | One pass through the terms, then closing questions             |

Studies with a Review round allow additional votes and comments after
Position. Study contributions retain their step context.

A permanent definition number identifies a candidate, regardless of screen
order. Use a [revision link](/docs/identifiers#citation) to cite exact text.

## When a study closes

Check the closing date on the study page. After that time, the activity
refuses new actions. Vocabulary work can continue outside the study and is
not counted retrospectively as study participation.

## Study administration

A steward or administrator prepares the collection, instructions, steps,
questions, and participation window. Candidate exclusions affect one study
and preserve vocabulary content and earlier responses. See
[Administration and stewardship](/docs/administration#studies).
