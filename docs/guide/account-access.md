# Account access

Select **Login** to contribute, vote, comment, or participate in a study.
The vocabulary and documentation are public. Use your existing account to
keep contributions and memberships together.

Begin sign-in from a study page to return there afterward. Existing members
can find activities under **Your studies** on their profile. See
[Studies](/docs/studies#the-study-page) for joining and participation.

## Verified email sign-in

Enter the email address attached to your account and select **Email me a
sign-in link**. Open the one-time link to sign in without a password. The
confirmation looks the same whether or not the address matches an account.

## Create an account with email

Select **Create an account with email** when available. Follow the email link,
then complete your name and profile choices. An invitation or study destination
is retained, including when you open the link on another device.

Use your existing account if you have contributed before. A second account
has separate contributions and memberships. Contact the person who invited
you if no available method lets you create an account.

## Continue with Google

Select **Continue with Google** and choose your usual account. Complete your
profile if requested before returning to the pending study or invitation.
Check the selected Google account if access is refused.

## ORCID

When ORCID access is enabled, sign in to your existing account, open
[Edit profile](/profile/edit), and select **Connect your ORCID iD**. Authorize
the connection at ORCID. After connecting, you can use **Continue with ORCID**
on the login page. Each iD can be connected to one MatSci-SAM account.

Stay signed in to the same account until the connection finishes. If you sign
out or change accounts during authorization, return to the intended profile
and start the connection again.

Sign-in with an unconnected iD opens email account creation when available.
Create your account first, then connect ORCID from your profile. Use your
existing account if you have contributed before. Accounts remain separate
after an ORCID connection.

Verify that you can sign in with Google or email before removing the connection.
Select **Disconnect** in **Edit profile**. Your contributions retain their
account attribution. ORCID display follows your profile visibility choice.

## Profile visibility

Profiles are private by default. Open [Edit profile](/profile/edit) and enable
**Public profile** to publish your name, affiliation, linked ORCID iD, and
contributed terms together. Email addresses and sign-in details remain private.

The setting controls access to your profile and links from your name. Your
name remains on contributions and provenance for attribution even with a
private profile.

## Trouble signing in

- Check spam and the email address if no sign-in message arrives. Request
  another link after the waiting period shown on the page.
- Request a new link if the previous one expired or was used. Open the most
  recent message.
- Check your account if studies or contributions are missing. Memberships and
  activity belong to the account used to create them.

Contact the person who shared the study or invitation if access remains
unavailable.

## Interface feedback

Use **Feedback** on any page to report a site problem, including while signed
out. It records your comment, page path, and submission time. Query parameters
and fragments are omitted. Submissions identify your account when you are
signed in. Others are recorded as Anonymous. Administrators can resolve and
reopen reports.
