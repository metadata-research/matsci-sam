This MRC demonstration uses four terms from the MRC pilot. It follows the study workflow in eleven steps.

For each term, the activity has two steps. The Position step shows definitions from earlier work. Choose the one closest to what you consider correct. Accept it as written, or suggest a revision. If none is close enough, propose a new definition. If you do not know a term well enough to choose, skip it. The Review step then shows the definitions for voting and comments. Vote on each, and comment where you disagree or can add information. Two questions complete the activity.

MatSci-SAM records the upvote used to accept a definition, any revised or new definitions, review votes, comments, and question responses. Completed steps are saved between visits. Contributions made through this study activity are recorded under the demonstration study and affect site-wide support.
