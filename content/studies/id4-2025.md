The 2025 MatSci-YAMZ study involved six participants affiliated with ID4. This collection contains the 19 unique term records present when the study closed, with the 20 human definitions and 19 Gemma 3 definitions reported in the paper. Participants reviewed and commented on definitions for their own terms and terms submitted by others.

The study ran from late July to mid-September 2025, with required tasks during two weeks in late August. The collection includes the seed terms discussed in the paper. The closed study reports activity at the end of the study window. Later activity remains available in the provenance for each term.

Greenberg et al. (2025), Human-in-the-Loop and AI: Crowdsourcing Metadata Vocabulary for Materials Science. arXiv:2512.09895.
