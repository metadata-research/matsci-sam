This 2025 study used MatSci-YAMZ with six participants affiliated with ID4. Together, they entered 20 terms relevant to their research domains, each with an initial definition and an illustrative example. Using Gemma 3 and the definitions and examples participants supplied, MatSci-YAMZ produced 19 AI-generated definitions. Participants commented on the AI-generated definitions for their own terms and for terms entered by others.

The broader testing period ran from late July to mid-September 2025, with specific tasks required during a two-week period late in August. MatSci-YAMZ had also been seeded with several terms before the study, and activity continued after the required-task period. The collection here is broader than the 20 participant-entered terms reported in the paper.

Greenberg et al. (2025), Human-in-the-Loop and AI: Crowdsourcing Metadata Vocabulary for Materials Science. arXiv:2512.09895.
